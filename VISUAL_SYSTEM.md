# Chef Lucas — visual system (Iris lock)

Quiet luxury. Editorial hospitality. Not SaaS, not carnival, not a fake star.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#1A1A1A` | Body text, solid buttons, header on cream |
| Soft black | `#0E0E0E` | Heroes, footer, demo banner |
| Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Page ground |
| Warm white | `#FCFAF6` | Cards, form fields, shop tiles |
| Gold | `#B8956A` | Jewellery only — rules, eyebrows, links, hairline accents |
| Stone | `#8A847A` | Captions, helper text |

Do not fill buttons or large surfaces with gold. No purple/teal SaaS, no neon glass.

## Type

- Display / menus / hero: **Newsreader** (Cormorant is an acceptable fallback)
- UI / forms: **Source Sans 3** (DM Sans is an acceptable fallback)
- Smaller, sharper body (15px). Tracking on eyebrows, not on paragraphs.

## Shape

- Cards and tiles: radius **8–12px** (we use 10px)
- Hairline borders (`rgba(14,14,14,0.12)`)
- No `rounded-full` marketing pills on cards
- Book wizard: calm, cream, hairline
- Shop: cream cards, not gold slabs

## Motion

- 400–700ms ease-out
- Fade + soft rise (`y: 10`)
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: static
- No bounce, no parallax carnival

## Photo

Real Chef Lucas kitchens from cheflucas.co.uk. Editorial still-life and brigade portraits. No stock waiters.

## Voice

Do not claim a Michelin star. The brief is a Michelin-grade *demo aesthetic*. Copy: fine dining hospitality, not “awarded”.
